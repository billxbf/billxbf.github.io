I'm a researcher at Nvidia, advancing RL systems for language agents -- infra, algorithms, data/env and harness, from a unified view.

I built [*Polar*](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/ProRL-Agent-Server), the first open RL framework bridging agent *post-training* and *harness engineering*.
To scale RL data for agents, [*Skill2Env*](https://github.com/NVlabs/Skill2Env) turns web-crawled *Agent Skills* into verifiable terminal tasks.
[*FlashREINFORCE*](https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2609.flashreinforce-asynchronous-rl-agentic-models) presents an elegant RL recipe to reduce instability and false credit-assignment in long-horizon async RL. 

My recent publications span terminal/computer-use agent, post-training infrastructure and algorithm, vertical harness (eg. autoresearch) and continual learning.
