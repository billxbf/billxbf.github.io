I'm a researcher at Nvidia advancing RL systems for agents, coupling training infra, recipe, data/env and harness design.

My recent study covers terminal/computer-use agent, post-training infrastructure and algorithm, vertical harness (eg. autoresearch) and continual learning.
For example, [Polar](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/ProRL-Agent-Server) is the first open RL framework realizing scalable *any-harness* rollout.
To scale agentic RL data in the open, [Skill2Env](https://github.com/NVlabs/Skill2Env) turns web-crawled *Agent Skills* into verifiable terminal tasks.
[FlashREINFORCE](https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2609.flashreinforce-asynchronous-rl-agentic-models) corrects false credit-assignment and train-inference mismatch to stablize long-horizon async RL.
