I'm focusing on advancing RL systems for language agents -- infra, algorithms, harness and data/env, optimized as a whole.

I built [*Polar*](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/ProRL-Agent-Server), the first open RL infra bridging LLM *post-training* and *harness engineering*.
[*Skill2Env*](https://github.com/NVlabs/Skill2Env) democratizes RL data scaling via collectively contributed *Skills*. 

My research area covers agentic post-training infrastructure and algorithms, terminal/computer-use agent, vertical harness (eg. autoresearch) and continual learning.
