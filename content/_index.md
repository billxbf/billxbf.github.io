I'm a researcher at Nvidia, co-optimizing RL systems for language agents -- infra, algorithms, harness and data/env.

For example, I built [*Polar*](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/ProRL-Agent-Server), the first open RL infra bridging LLM *post-training* and *harness engineering*.
To scale RL data in the open, [*Skill2Env*](https://github.com/NVlabs/Skill2Env) synthesizes environments with public *Agent Skills*.
[*FlashREINFORCE*](https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2609.flashreinforce-asynchronous-rl-agentic-models) presents an elegant RL recipe to reduce instability and false credit-assignment in long, async RL. 

See below for recent publications on terminal/computer-use agent, post-training infrastructure and algorithm, vertical harness (eg. autoresearch) and continual learning.
