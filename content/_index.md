I'm a researcher at Nvidia, focusing on advancing RL systems for language agents -- infra, algorithms, harness and data/env, optimized as a whole.

For example, I built [*Polar*](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/ProRL-Agent-Server), the first open RL infra bridging LLM *post-training* and *harness engineering*.
To scale RL data in the open, [*Skill2Env*](https://github.com/NVlabs/Skill2Env) synthesize environments with public *Agent Skills*.
[*FlashREINFORCE*](https://www.alphaxiv.org/pdf/2609.flashreinforce-asynchronous-rl-agentic-models) presents a simple and elegant RL recipe to mitigate instability and false credit-assignment in long-run async RL. 

Check below for recent publication on terminal/computer-use agent, post-training infrastructure and algorithms, vertical harness (eg. autoresearch) and continual learning.
