I'm focusing on advancing RL systems for Agents -- infra, algorithms, data scaling and harness, in a unified perspective.

For example, I built [*Polar*](https://github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/ProRL-Agent-Server), the first open framework bridging LLM *post-training* and *harness engineering*.
[*Skill2Env*](https://github.com/NVlabs/Skill2Env) democratizes RL data scaling via collective *Skills*.

My research covers RL systems and algorithms, terminal/computer-use agent, harness design and continual learning.
I believe AGI is grokking of human-shared values and interests, and thus the internals must be transparent to all.
